The two formats

Sprint race. Everyone gets the same problems in the same order and answers as many as possible before time runs out. The projector shows a live race.

Rounds. One problem at a time, like Kahoot. Faster correct answers earn more points (1000 down to 500). After each round the projector shows the answer, the fastest students and the standings.

Every finished game is saved in History with each student's score.
